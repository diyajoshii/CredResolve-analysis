--- dim_agent  [QUARANTINED] ---------------------------------------------
  30,000 rows, 1,000 agent_id, 1,099 employee_code.
  Every agent_id carries 9.5 different agent_names on average.
  agent_id <-> employee_code forms ONE connected component of 2,099 nodes.
  CONCLUSION: there is no natural key and no resolvable person entity in this table.
  DECISION: build dim_agent as an SCD-1 collapse on agent_id (latest updated_at) so joins
            do not fan out, but mark the whole dimension trust_level='LOW' and publish NO
            agent-level or tenure-level metric from it. Anything the current reporting says
            about 'top agents' or 'agent tenure' is unsupported by this data.
  COUNTER-EXAMPLE: resolving on employee_code (the obvious 'natural key') would raise mean
            calls-per-person from 88 to 137 (+56%) — a 56% productivity 'improvement'
            created entirely by a bad entity-resolution choice.
  [agents                ] SCD-1 collapse on agent_id               30,000 ->    1,000  (29,000 removed)  join-safety only; NOT for reporting

--- fct_disposition ------------------------------------------------------
  [call_dispositions     ] dedup                                    35,000 ->   35,000  (     0 removed)  surrogate key uniqueness
  Code harmonisation: 9 raw codes -> 8 standard outcomes.
     PTP + PROMISE_TO_PAY -> PROMISE_TO_PAY   (the same outcome under legacy and v1/v2 taxonomies)
     PTP_BROKEN           -> PROMISE_BROKEN
  PTP rate on the literal code 'PTP' = 11.2%; on the harmonised outcome = 22.4%.
  => any dashboard matching disposition_code='PTP' undercounts promises by 50%.
  50% of dispositions are timestamped BEFORE their parent call.
  DECISION: dispositions are NOT treated as children of calls. calls/attempts/dispositions are
            three independent event streams that share a call_id label but not a consistent
            clock. RPC is measured on the disposition stream in its own right, never as a
            ratio to the call stream.

--- fct_ptp --------------------------------------------------------------
  [promises_to_pay       ] dedup                                    18,000 ->   18,000  (     0 removed)  surrogate key uniqueness
  0 promises are due before they were made (flagged).
  DECISION: PTP-kept rate denominator = RESOLVED promises only (KEPT+BROKEN+CANCELLED).
     kept / all promises        = 24.9%   <- understates: OPEN promises can still be kept

--- fct_touch (unified interaction stream) -------------------------------
  219,948 interactions unified across VOICE / WHATSAPP / SMS / FIELD.
  'engaged' is defined per channel as a two-way signal (answered / read-replied-clicked /
  clicked / met-in-person), NOT as 'delivered'. Delivery is a vendor metric, not a
  borrower-engagement metric, and inflating it is the easiest way to fake a contact rate.

--- fct_targeting --------------------------------------------------------
  [daily_targeting       ] dedup                                    45,000 ->   45,000  (     0 removed)  surrogate key uniqueness
  LIFECYCLE TEST: 19,673 status changes occur AFTER an account's first terminal status,
     and their distribution is uniform: {'DELINQUENT': 0.146, 'PAID': 0.146, 'WRITEOFF': 0.144, 'CLOSED': 0.143, 'NPA': 0.142, 'ACTIVE': 0.141, 'PTP': 0.139}
  => account_status_history is NOT a state machine. CLOSED/WRITEOFF/PAID are not absorbing
     states; accounts revert. 'first terminal event' is therefore an invalid definition of
     account closure and must not be used. We use LAST KNOWN status as-of the target date.

  10,991 of 45,000 targeting rows (24.4%) aim at an
  account whose LAST KNOWN status at that moment was CLOSED / WRITEOFF / PAID.
     naive monthly trend            : {Period('2026-01', 'M'): 5.7, Period('2026-02', 'M'): 13.9, Period('2026-03', 'M'): 20.8, Period('2026-04', 'M'): 26.7, Period('2026-05', 'M'): 30.5, Period('2026-06', 'M'): 33.7, Period('2026-07', 'M'): 35.4, Period('2026-08', 'M'): 39.0}
     restricted to accounts with a known status:
                                     : {Period('2026-01', 'M'): 43.0, Period('2026-02', 'M'): 42.5, Period('2026-03', 'M'): 42.6, Period('2026-04', 'M'): 43.6, Period('2026-05', 'M'): 43.3, Period('2026-06', 'M'): 43.4, Period('2026-07', 'M'): 42.4, Period('2026-08', 'M'): 45.2}
  TRAP AVOIDED: the naive series rises 6% -> 58% and looks like operations degrading badly.
     It is not. Early months simply have little status history, so most accounts read as
     'status unknown'. Among accounts with a known status the figure is FLAT at ~43%.
     Reporting the naive series would have handed leadership a fake crisis.
  BENCHMARK: 43.2% of targeting hits a terminal account. If targeting ignored
     status entirely, random selection would hit 42.9% (3 of 7 statuses).
     The targeting engine is statistically indistinguishable from random on account status.
  DECISION: these rows STAY in the golden layer (they are real operational spend) but are
            flagged. This is the largest genuine operational finding in the dataset.
